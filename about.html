HELLO, I'M JOHN CHRIS

A creative mind.
A strategic thinker.

I'm John Chris Broqueza, a multidisciplinary creative professional with 8 years of experience in graphic design, video editing, social media management, and digital content creation.

Over the years, I've worked with businesses across different industries, helping them communicate their message through engaging visuals, compelling videos, and creative marketing strategies.

My experience goes beyond making things look good. I understand how creativity and business goals work together. From producing social media campaigns and promotional videos to developing visual identities and websites, I approach every project with both creativity and purpose.

I enjoy learning new tools, exploring emerging technologies, and finding better ways to transform ideas into meaningful experiences.
